Merge matching tiles to reach 2048—or go even higher. Click the board to focus it, then use the arrow keys or WASD to move the tiles. You can also swipe on the board or use the direction buttons below it.

![iframe: 2048 Mini-Game](./2048.html)

You can undo up to five moves and import or export saved games as JSON files.

Export your saved game before leaving this page.

To resume later, import the JSON file to restore your board, score, and undo history.
